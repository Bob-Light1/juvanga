# Juvanga — visual identity proposal 02

English text translation of [JUVANGA.pdf](../JUVANGA.pdf), one page. Historical proposal; see the [current brand guidelines](../../design/BRAND.md) for adopted choices.

**Juvanga — Illuminate Your Potential.**

Direction: “Light that opens the future.” A rising sun, symbolizing knowledge and momentum, in a warm, deep palette. More institutional and versatile than FURAKILI; because the name does not evoke AI, it should always be accompanied by “Practical AI Learning”.

## Four logo directions

- **A — Rising sun over an open book.** A half-sun rises over book pages: jua (sun) and -jua (to know) in one symbol. The clearest training direction. Legible from 32 px; simplify rays below that size.
- **B — J monogram + sun.** A generously curved J whose dot is a sun. Compact, ideal for an app icon, favicon and profile photo. Combine with the word in lockups.
- **C — Sun behind the word.** A golden disc passes behind the J, underlined by an orange stroke. Simplest to produce and most typographic; works well in one color.
- **D — Solar emblem and tagline.** A twelve-ray sun above the uppercase name, with the explicit tagline “PRACTICAL AI LEARNING”. An institutional vertical lockup for home pages, certificates and presentations.

## Typography and sample copy

Headings: **Fraunces**. “Let your skills rise.” A warm, confident serif for an authoritative tone.

Body: **Inter**. “Practical training in artificial intelligence, designed for people, teams and organizations.” Restrained, highly legible, equally comfortable on mobile and printed documents.
