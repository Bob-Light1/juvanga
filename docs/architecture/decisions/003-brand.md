# ADR-003 — Juvanga identity

Date: 2026-11-06. Status: adopted within the logo selection assignment.

The user confirmed Juvanga. Visual comparison of the four original PNGs: B chosen as the primary symbol because it is compact and legible on mobile; A is more school-oriented and detailed, C mainly typographic, D too tall for navigation. The choice prioritizes the application and future extension.

The lockup reuses monogram B and vectorized lettering from proposal A. Provenance is retained in manifest.json; sources are not replaced. Canonical colors come from JUVANGA.pdf, supplemented by explicit contrast variants. Night blue, sand, gold, orange and green; never gold small text on a light background.

Consequences: one active product family; others remain archived. Legal distinctiveness of the name/symbol and licenses are not established by this visual choice. Test recognition at 24/32 px and contrast in the workshop; revision is possible before launch without changing historical documents.
