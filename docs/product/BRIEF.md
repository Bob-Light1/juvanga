# Product brief — concise reference

User decision on 6 November 2026: name **Juvanga**. Working tagline, translated: **Learn. Practise. Progress.** French launch copy: “Apprendre. Pratiquer. Progresser.”

## Promise

Enable a person or team to perform useful tasks through practical, guided learning. The first courses cover LLMs and office productivity, followed by websites and web applications. The model may cover AI, development and science; domain or level never automatically determines price.

## Audiences and expected outcomes

| Audience | Primary need | Evidence of value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner or professional | Understand and apply without intimidation | Complete a task from their work |
| Guided learner | Receive corrections and overcome blockers | Deliverable accepted against a known rubric |
| Trainer/editor | Teach, present and assess | Consistent course/version and criteria |
| Organization | Train a cohort | Allocated seats and authorized progress reporting |
| Operations/finance | Schedule sessions and reconcile receipts | Auditable transactions and decisions |

## Invariants

- Reading a lesson does not establish competence. Distinguish self-assessment, human review and certificates.
- Level 0: explicitly public resources. Paid courses: controlled entitlements even when the URL is known.
- Verify external payment before paid access; booking is independent. Lack of a slot does not remove an acquired reading entitlement.
- Version offers, content and rubrics. Every order retains its price and terms.
- Check roles and organization context on the server; minimize financial data.
- French at launch; XAF amounts; Cameroonian sessions displayed in Africa/Douala.

## Differentiation to build and measure

1. **A visible result in every lesson:** a document, analysis or completed task, with clear criteria.
2. **A lightweight mobile experience:** text first, optional videos, progress recoverable on a personal device.
3. **Continuity between learning and guidance:** resume the same content during a session or presentation.
4. **An African identity open to the world:** real local examples, clear vocabulary, multilingual architecture.
5. **Trust:** understandable payment states, retained versions, justified progress and accessible support.

These are hypotheses to test, not proven competitive advantages. Avoid public rankings, punitive streaks, fictional reviews and unsubstantiated certification claims.

## Measures

M0: independent completion of the first task, time to first result, understanding the course journey and data usage. M1: return rate, exercise submission and review turnaround. M2: reconciliation turnaround, time to a session, disputes and cost per active learner. Start with consented interviews and feedback; no mandatory third-party tracker.
