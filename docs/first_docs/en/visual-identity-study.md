# Earlier visual identity study — direction 1 of 2

English text translation of [the supplied visual study](<../Identités visuelles Furakili et Juvanga.pdf>), one page. Despite the source filename, its page presents the Furakili direction. Earlier palette alternatives are historical; do not merge them into the active Juvanga palette.

**furakili — Intelligence Meets Joy.**

Direction: AI without intimidation. Warm, vivid colors on a cream background, rounded, confident typography and a smiling symbol that never feels childish. The tone remains demanding: joy comes from mastery.

## 01 — Palette

The contrast values below are claims in the source proposal, not new measurements.

## 02 — Typography

Headings: **Outfit**. “Learn with pleasure.” Section heading: 28–36 px, 700. Label: 14 px, spaced uppercase.

Body: **DM Sans**. “Every lesson ends with a concrete exercise. You see your skills progress, one by one, without jargon.” Body 17–18 px, line height 1.5. Minimum 16 px on mobile. Error messages offer help, never blame.

Name rule: lowercase “furakili”, Outfit 700, tight kerning.

## 03 — Three logo directions

- **A — Spark.** An F in three bars, like three skill levels, and an idea spark. Most direct and memorable.
- **B — Smile and progress.** A smile containing a progress curve. Joy and assessment in one symbol, legible even when tiny.
- **C — Idea bubble.** A speech bubble carrying a spark: guidance and conversational AI. Warmest of the three.

Each direction is shown with “furakili — Intelligence Meets Joy.”, plus app-icon and reduced-size examples.
