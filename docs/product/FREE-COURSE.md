# M0 free course — meeting notes to an action plan

Ticket: JUV-101. Date: 2026-11-07. Status: plan approved by the project owner on 2026-11-07; course not published.

## Outcome and audience

A beginner will turn a short set of fictional meeting notes into a concise summary and an action plan showing what must happen, who is responsible, when it is due and what still needs clarification. The result is a reusable office document that a colleague can act on.

Proposed French product title: “Transformer des notes de réunion en plan d’action clair.” English meaning: “Turn meeting notes into a clear action plan.” This is a product-copy example; learner-facing French content belongs in the catalogue prepared by JUV-107 and the course authored in JUV-103.

The course is free, public and level 0. It requires basic reading and typing, a phone or computer with a browser, and access to the lesson text. A learner can complete the activity on paper or in a text editor. No spreadsheet software, paid tool, AI provider or account is required. The initial reading experience needs a connection; offline behavior remains subject to JUV-112 testing.

Estimated learning time: 35–50 minutes, including a final check. This is a planning hypothesis to validate with beginners in JUV-113, not an observed completion time.

## Three-lesson sequence

| Lesson | Observable objective | Example and guided practice | Expected answer | Planned time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Separate facts, decisions and open questions | Classify a note without turning an assumption into a fact | Compare “eight laptops are available” with “a room has been requested”; classify N1, N2 and N6 below | N1 is an agreed topic; N2 is a confirmed resource; N6 is unresolved, with no confirmed room | 8–12 minutes |

Make actions executable | Write an action with a verb, owner and deadline, preserving missing information | Work through N3, then transform N4 and N5 using the same fields | Check chargers / logistics lead / 17 November at 12:00; draft invitation / communications lead / 18 November at 16:00; review invitation / facilitator / 19 November at 12:00 | 10–15 minutes |

Produce and verify a shareable plan | Assemble the summary, action list and clarification question; check every statement against its source | Complete the final exercise from all six notes; compare with the worked solution only after attempting it | A short summary, four action rows including the unresolved venue follow-up, and a question identifying the missing owner, deadline and venue confirmation | 12–18 minutes |

## Final exercise — original fictional source

Scenario: a small team in Douala is preparing a free office-productivity workshop. All roles, events and notes below are fictional. Use only these notes; no real names, contact details or business records are needed.

Deadlines below are stored as UTC instants. Local display uses Africa/Douala. The display examples are in English for this planning document; JUV-103/JUV-107 supply the French learner copy.

| Source | Meeting note |
| --- | --- |
| N1 | The team agreed that the workshop topic will be spreadsheet basics. |
| N2 | Eight shared laptops are available for sixteen learners, working in pairs. |
| N3 | The logistics lead will check the laptop chargers by `2026-11-17T11:00:00Z` (17 November 2026 at 12:00 in Douala). |
| N4 | The communications lead will draft the invitation by `2026-11-18T15:00:00Z` (18 November 2026 at 16:00 in Douala). The invitation must remain a draft until reviewed. |
| N5 | The facilitator will review the invitation by `2026-11-19T11:00:00Z` (19 November 2026 at 12:00 in Douala). |
| N6 | A venue has been requested. No room is confirmed. No one has been assigned to follow up, and no follow-up deadline has been agreed. |

### Learner deliverable

Prepare a short document containing:

1. A summary of at most two sentences stating the topic, available equipment and unresolved venue.
2. An action list with the fields **Action**, **Owner**, **Deadline** and **Status**. Include the three assigned tasks and a venue follow-up clearly marked as needing assignment.
3. A clarification question about the missing venue information.
4. A completed self-assessment against the criteria below, after comparing the result with the worked solution.

Keep the invitation as a draft; do not invent a workshop date, confirmed room, assigned venue contact or follow-up deadline. Typing the answer does not submit it to Juvanga. M0 assesses the learner's own work; it does not certify competence. Exercise text must not be persisted by the eventual application; optional reading progress is a separate JUV-110 capability.

### Worked solution

Summary:

> The team is preparing a spreadsheet-basics workshop for sixteen learners using eight shared laptops in pairs. The venue is still unconfirmed, and the invitation must remain a draft until reviewed.

| Action | Owner | Deadline displayed in Africa/Douala | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Check the laptop chargers | Logistics lead | 17 November 2026 at 12:00 | Assigned; completion not reported |
| Draft the invitation | Communications lead | 18 November 2026 at 16:00 | Assigned; keep as draft pending review |
| Review the invitation | Facilitator | 19 November 2026 at 12:00 | Assigned; completion not reported |
| Follow up on the venue request | To assign | To agree | Follow-up proposed from an unresolved issue; room unconfirmed |

Clarification question:

> Who will follow up on the venue request, by when, and how will the team confirm which room is available?

The last row is explicitly a proposed next action, not an assignment already agreed in the meeting. Equivalent wording and a plain-text list are acceptable if the same information is preserved. Learners do not need a table editor.

### Observable self-assessment

| Criterion | Observable evidence | If missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faithfulness | The topic, sixteen learners and eight laptops match N1–N2; no unsupported commitment appears | Return to lesson 1 and compare each claim with the notes |
| Action clarity | Three assigned tasks and the proposed venue follow-up are separate, with actionable verbs | Return to lesson 2 and split vague or combined tasks |
| Responsibility | The three recorded roles are correct; venue responsibility is explicitly unassigned | Restore the source role or mark the missing owner |
| Deadline accuracy | All three deadlines match N3–N5 in Douala time; no venue deadline is invented | Compare with the source and mark the missing deadline |
| Honest status | No task is claimed complete, no room is claimed confirmed, and the invitation remains a draft | Correct the status using N3–N6 |
| Useful clarification | The question asks for a venue follow-up owner, deadline and confirmation | Make the missing information explicit |

All six criteria must be self-checked before describing the document as ready to share in this exercise. A failed check prompts revision and another comparison, not a competence score or certificate. Reading the solution does not establish mastery.
