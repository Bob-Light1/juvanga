# Launch offer catalogue — AI at work

Ticket: JUV-116. Decision: [DEC-15](DECISIONS.md#dec-15-launch-offer--2026-11-27), 2026-11-27. Status: structure, durations, follow-up and launch prices approved by the project owner for planning and public presentation. **Nothing is on sale yet**: real sales still require the commercial policies of JUV-301 (DEC-01 to DEC-05), a named second finance operator and the paid-access mechanisms of M1/M2.

French titles below are working product copy with their English meaning; final learner-facing wording belongs in the i18n catalogue.

## Focus and audience

Launch with a single domain: using generative AI assistants for everyday professional work. Primary audience: executives and administrators of public, private and parapublic organizations, and groups of employees or members of organizations. Other learners are welcome. Websites, applications, AI agent development and marketing are deferred to a creator track opened only after observed demand.

## Common rules

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Group pricing | One rule for every paid offer: −15% per person for 5 to 9 participants, −25% for a group of 10. Group totals may be rounded to a readable amount. |

| Titles | State the outcome, not a vendor. Tools are listed in the description and pages state that Juvanga is not affiliated with the vendors named. |

## Content visibility and paid access

Visitors see each offer and its information. Course content is available only to people whose payment has been verified and approved. Paid access follows the rule in [SECURITY.md](../architecture/SECURITY.md):

1. A person other than the approver records the payment after checking the merchant or Mobile Money statement.
2. The project owner approves and grants access.
3. A screenshot alone never grants access.

The second operator is not yet named (DEC-04). Until M2 exists, in-person sales use manual quotes and invoices, and paid online content cannot be delivered on the platform.

## Capacity estimate

This is a planning hypothesis, not a measurement.

| --- | --- |

| Follow-up for one N1 group, per month, over 3 months | 10 hours individually (10 learners × 1 hour), plus 2 group sessions of about 1.5 hours = about 13 hours |

## Open points

- Leaders flat fee; community licence for organizations; whether the community is included during each paid course's follow-up period.
- Whether N2 includes an offered subscription. Building custom assistants may require a paid plan.
- Whether each module's outline is public. It is recommended because public buyers need a detailed programme before ordering.
- A completion attestation after a human-reviewed final project. If adopted, it must never be presented as an official diploma or certification.
