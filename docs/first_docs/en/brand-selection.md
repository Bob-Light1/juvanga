## Page 1 — Three names. Three visions. One brand to choose.

African origins, brand stories, meanings and arguments for a collective decision.

**JUVANGA · FURAKILI · LUBUTEXA**

Positioning study — November 2026.

## Page 2 — Preamble and method

### Supporting a collective choice

Three identities to choose between. A document to read, share and annotate, without presenting a marketing hypothesis as a linguistic fact.

Objective: identify a name that inspires confidence, carries an authentic story, remains easy to pronounce and can sustainably support an African training and artificial-intelligence platform.

Three levels of interpretation:

1. **Linguistic roots:** authentic words with documented meanings in their respective languages.
2. **Brand creation:** freely combined sound fragments; the final name is not necessarily a dictionary word.
3. **Symbolic vision:** values and promises the company deliberately associates with the name.

The three directions in one sentence:

- **JUVANGA:** illuminate possibilities: energy, knowledge, radiance.
- **FURAKILI:** make intelligence accessible, stimulating and joyful.
- **LUBUTEXA:** connect the light of knowledge with technology.

## Page 3 — Juvanga identity

### Light that opens the future

**JUVANGA — Illuminate Your Potential.**

Guiding idea: a brand focused on momentum, discovery and the reach of human skills.

A construction inspired by two African languages:

- **Swahili — Jua:** sun. Swahili also has the verb -jua: “to know”.
- **Zulu — Ilanga:** sun; also associated with day and daylight.

Word creation: **JU** (from Jua) + **V** (invented connection) + **ANGA** (inspired by Ilanga).

What the name evokes: a symbolic sun born from two African horizons: the light of knowledge, the ability to act and openness to the world.

Clarification: “Juvanga” is a brand neologism. It is not a literal translation of a Swahili or Zulu expression. Linguistic references [S1] and [S2].

## Page 4 — Juvanga story and positioning

Proposed text for a roughly one-minute speech:

> We imagined Juvanga by drawing inspiration from two African languages. In Swahili, jua means sun; in Zulu, ilanga also refers to the sun.
>
> We brought these inspirations together in a new word to express a conviction: knowledge should illuminate everyone's possibilities.
>
> We want artificial intelligence to become a new source of ability, independence and creation, rather than an intimidating tool. Juvanga carries this African ambition open to the world.

Three ideas to defend:

- **Reach:** grow skills so they benefit people, teams and organizations.
- **Energy:** associate learning with movement, visible progress and concrete results.
- **Openness:** create a brand relevant beyond AI and initial training.

Point to watch: the name does not explain the activity by itself. Always accompany it with an explicit tagline, for example “Practical AI Learning”.

## Page 5 — Furakili identity

### Intelligence that makes you want to learn

**FURAKILI — Intelligence Meets Joy.**

Guiding idea: make discovering AI a positive, reassuring and concrete experience, whatever the person's initial digital experience.

Two Swahili roots:

- **Furaha:** joy, happiness, pleasure in learning.
- **Akili:** intelligence, reason, mind, ability to understand.

Word creation: **FUR** (beginning of Furaha) + **AKILI** (retained word).

What the name evokes: intelligence without intimidation: understand, practise, succeed and enjoy progress.

Clarification: “Furakili” is an invented fusion. It does not literally mean “joyful intelligence” in Swahili; the isolated fragment “fura” may have other meanings. References [S3] and [S4].

## Page 6 — Furakili story and positioning

Proposed text for a roughly one-minute speech:

> Furakili draws inspiration from two Swahili words: furaha, joy, and akili, intelligence. We wanted a name reminding us that knowledge should not be frightening.
>
> Many professionals still hesitate to use artificial intelligence because they fear making mistakes or not understanding. Our approach is to progress through practice, build confidence and see the skills being acquired.
>
> Furakili is the meeting of the pleasure of learning and the ability to do.

Three ideas to defend:

- **Accessibility:** remove intimidating conventions and progress with understandable explanations.
- **Trust:** show that every difficulty can become a mastered skill.
- **Pleasure in progress:** value exercises, independence and learners' small victories.

Point to watch: warmth helps beginners, but communication must also demonstrate educational rigor and business credibility.

## Page 7 — Lubutexa identity

### Knowledge meets technology

**LUBUTEXA — Where Knowledge Meets Technology.**

Guiding idea: connect human knowledge with tools capable of transforming how people learn, work and create.

A Bemba root and an invented ending:

- **Bemba — Lubuto:** “light”; by extension, evokes knowledge and awakening.
- **Created — Texa:** invented sound sequence evoking modern technology.

Word creation: **LUBU** (beginning of Lubuto) + **TEXA** (invented ending).

What the name evokes: the light of knowledge set in motion by innovation: technology serving real human abilities.

Clarification: “Texa” is neither a Bemba word nor an official abbreviation for “technology”. Lubutexa is invented; “lubuto” is the documented root. References [S5] and [S6].

## Page 8 — Lubutexa story and positioning

Proposed text for a roughly one-minute speech:

> Lubutexa was born from the meeting of African inspiration and technological ambition. The Bemba word lubuto refers to light, an image also associated with knowledge and awakening.
>
> We retained part of that word and created the ending Texa to form a new identity.
>
> Our idea is simple: technology has greater value when it helps people understand, learn and produce independently. We want innovation to serve mastery and independence.

Three ideas to defend:

- **Rigor:** associate technical modernity with structured, assessed training.
- **Independence:** turn technological tools into usable skills.
- **Scalability:** support a platform, software products or consulting in the future.

Point to watch: Lubuto Library Partners already uses “Lubuto” in education and digital resources. Check similarities and avoid suggesting affiliation. Source [S7].

## Page 11 — Arguments and frequently asked questions

Six short answers for interviews or meetings:

- **Why a name inspired by African languages?** To affirm African cultural inspiration while building a product open to the world.
- **Is the name an authentic expression?** No. The cited roots are authentic; each final name is a brand creation.
- **Why not choose an English name?** International identity also rests on pronunciation, product quality and communication.
- **Why connect the brand with AI?** The ambition is to make intelligent tools understandable and useful, then gradually broaden the skills taught.
- **What exactly does the selected name mean?** Distinguish each root's precise meaning from the brand's deliberately chosen symbolic message.
- **What will make the company different?** Practical learning: users progress when they can apply what they learned.

Do not claim without evidence: “This word means exactly…” when only symbolic interpretation exists; “We are the only ones using it”; “The domain and trademark are available”. Any personal creation anecdote must be true.

### Recommended checks before adoption

- **Domain:** check current .com, .ai and other relevant extensions with registries and registrars.
- **Trademark:** search OAPI for identical and similar names, particularly for training and software activities.
- **Reception:** test pronunciation and recall with French and English speakers without showing spelling first.
- **Decision:** select a name, establish official pronunciation, then create the logo, social accounts and site.

One more question: which name would you be proud to recommend to a colleague, say aloud and see on an application used in several countries?
