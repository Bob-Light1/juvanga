# Furakili — visual identity proposal 01

English text translation of [FURAKILI.pdf](../FURAKILI.pdf), one page. Historical alternative; the selected product name is Juvanga.

**furakili — Intelligence Meets Joy.**

Direction: “Intelligence without intimidation.” A warm, rounded, luminous brand grounded in serious indigo to retain business credibility. Always accompanied by the explicit tagline “Practical AI Training”.

## Four logo directions

- **A — F monogram + joy dot.** An F built from rounded bars, punctuated by a yellow dot (the sun of joy). Split the word into “fura” (Furaha) and “kili” (Akili), following its name origin. The safest, most adaptable direction.
- **B — Smiling spark.** A four-point idea spark and a smile inside a solar disc: intelligence that attracts. More expressive and consumer-oriented; reserve for educational materials and social media.
- **C — Two meeting circles.** Furaha (coral) and Akili (yellow) overlap; the indigo intersection represents the outcome: an acquired skill. Spaced uppercase letters, a more corporate and reassuring B2B tone.
- **D — Wordmark with tagline.** The word alone, without a symbol, underlined in yellow and followed by “PRACTICAL AI TRAINING”, clarifying the activity. Useful in email banners, invoice headers and contractual documents.

Headings: **Outfit**. “Learn AI with a smile.” Weight 600 for subheadings, 700 for headings.

Body: **DM Sans**. “Short, concrete, progressive courses: each module ends with a result you can show.” Legible at small sizes, neutral, renders very well on screen.
